# Methodology
Fixed seed 20261116. Exactly 200 synthetic outlets across nine environments and multiple Bangladesh districts. Outlet scale affects assortment and customer profile. 260 Bangladesh-relevant product catalogue rows include local staple and daily-necessity categories. Assortments are probabilistically weighted by market environment; not every outlet stocks every product. Sales are sparse daily outlet-product-batch aggregates with stochastic quantities, discounts and weekday variation, capped by batch stock and expiry. Perishables receive monthly batches; longer-life goods quarterly batches. Purchases and purchase-order items map to those batches. Wastage is deducted from remaining stock. Daily metrics are computed from sales. Calendar windows use official 2026 holiday dates where published; lunar dates are provisional and synthetic demand effects are configurable assumptions.
