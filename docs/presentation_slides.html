AI & Machine Learning

67 SAFE Feature Machine Learning Demand Engine

Dual-horizon HistGradientBoosting regression for precision ordering

🛒 HAAT-BAZAAR

🔬 67 SAFE Feature Pipeline

  • Sales Momentum (19 features): 1d, 2d, 3d, 7d, 14d, 28d lags, rolling standard deviations, and non-zero sales day frequency.
  • Inventory Dynamics (14 features): Stock-to-sales ratios, expiring stock in 7d/30d, days since restock, reorder points.
  • Outlet Demographics (8 features): Business size, area type (residential, commercial, university hub), refrigeration capacity.
  • Calendar & Culture (12 features): Day of week, month, payday cycles, and religious festivals.

📈 Dual-Horizon Models

7-Day Tactical Horizon (EXP3_7D):

Guides short-cycle perishable ordering (milk, eggs, vegetables, bread) to minimize spoilage.

30-Day Strategic Horizon (HGB_30D):

Optimizes bulk dry goods (rice, lentils, oil, spices) to secure wholesale supplier discounts.

Smart Purchase Quantity (ROQ):

Formula: ROQ = Forecasted_Demand + Safety_Stock - Current_Stock
